Privacy Policy

SOQQA.uz — Nasiya Daftari is a debt and credit management tool for small business owners. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Ushbu ilova kichik biznes egalari uchun nasiya va qarz boshqaruv vositasidir.

Data Collection · Ma'lumotlar

Information We Collect

  • Your phone number and password (used for account login)
  • Customer names and phone numbers you enter manually
  • Debt amounts and due dates you record in the app
  • SMS sending history (reminders sent to customers)

We do not collect location data, device contacts, or payment information.

Data Use · Foydalanish

How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate your account and keep your data secure
  • To display your customer debt records within the app
  • To send SMS payment reminders on your behalf (via native iOS SMS)
  • To generate reports visible only to you

We do not sell your data to third parties or use it for advertising.

Third-Party Services · Xizmatlar

External Services Used

  • Native iOS SMS — sends payment reminders directly from your device
  • Telegram — a deep link opens Telegram to message the customer; no data is shared automatically

No analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or third-party data processors are used.

Your Rights · Huquqlaringiz

Data Deletion & Account

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. We will process your request within 7 business days.
